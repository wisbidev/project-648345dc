About me

Designer by craft, problem-solver by instinct.

I'm Minh Nguyen, a product designer based in Ho Chi Minh City. For the past eight years I've helped startups and product teams take ideas from a napkin sketch to a shipped, loved product — across fintech, health, and education.

My approach is simple: listen hard, design simply, test early. I believe great design is invisible — it removes friction so people can focus on what actually matters to them, not on the interface in front of them.

When I'm not designing, I'm teaching a UX workshop, photographing street corners, or hunting down the best bánh mì in the city.
